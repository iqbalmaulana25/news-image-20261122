One dense broadsheet front page summing up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026 in **36 dated stories across 10 sections and 7 regions**: the COP31 climate talks collapse in Antalya leads, with the Benghazi sea-drone strike on a Russian warship and the strikes on Kyiv beside it; then Europe, Middle East, Asia-Pacific, Africa, Americas, Business & Markets, Technology, Science & Health and Culture columns, a "Day in Numbers" strip and a Sport band (Alcaraz's first ATP Finals title, Arsenal's derby win, India's 720/9 at Wellington). Every story was reported on the day itself. The image is `entry.png` (JPEG bytes under a .png name, which the gate allows); `main.ts` checks its format and copies it to the output directory when the entry runs.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
